# Glossary

One spelling per name or term, used everywhere in the patch. Where the
arcade's own English already names something, we follow it, except for
obvious typos.

In-game text is plain ASCII, so no macrons (ō, ū): "Muso Tensei", not
"Musō Tensei".

## Style notes

- **Timeline events**: short present-tense headlines ("Kenshiro meets Lin and
  Bat."). Each line is at most 47 characters.
- **Quotes**: natural spoken English that keeps the manga's punch. Keep the
  double "!!" where the original has it. Iconic lines keep their iconic
  English ("Hidebu!!", "Say my name!", "I have no regrets in my life!!").
- **Save messages**: the Japanese is all hiragana, written like a little kid
  talking. The English is all lowercase, cutesy and drawn-out
  ("loadiiing~").
- Never use `@` or `%` in text (`@` is the arcade's line break code, `%` is
  a format code).
